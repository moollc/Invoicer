Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

mooInvoicer ("the app") is an invoicing tool that runs in your web browser at https://invoicer.moo.llc. It is made and run by moo.llc ("we"). This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app uses, why, where it is kept, and how you can remove it.

The short version

  • The app has no server of its own. Your invoices and settings live in your browser and, if you connect Google, in your own Google Drive and Google Sheets.
  • We do not receive, store, sell, or share your data. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking.
  • Google access is optional. You choose to connect it, and you can revoke it at any time.

Who runs the app

mooInvoicer is run by moo.llc. To contact us, use the Contact developer button at the bottom of this page. We do not publish an email address.

Data you enter

When you use the app you type in business details, client names and contact details, line items, amounts, payments, notes, bills, goals, and settings. This data is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. If you connect Google, a copy is also kept in your own Google Sheet (see below).

Google data the app uses, and why

Signing in with Google is optional. When you choose Sign in with Google, the app asks Google for these permissions (OAuth scopes), all on one consent screen:

PermissionWhat the app does with it
Gmail, read only
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly		 Only when you press the button to scan Gmail for bill payments: the app searches your mail for payment, receipt, and invoice emails that match the names of bills you track in the app (for example a bank alert or a service receipt). It reads the matching emails' sender, subject, date, and text to find the amount paid, and shows you the matches to accept or skip. If you accept one, it may download that email's receipt attachment (a PDF or image) and save it to your Drive with the bill. When you sign in, it also reads your Gmail address so Google can pre-select your account the next time you sign in. It never changes, deletes, or labels your email.
Gmail, compose drafts
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.compose		 When you choose to email an invoice, the app creates a draft in your Gmail addressed to your client, with the invoice PDF attached. It does not send it. You review and send the draft yourself in Gmail.
Google Drive, files the app creates or you open with it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file		 The app creates a folder named "mooInvoicer" in your Drive and saves invoice PDF backups, receipts you attach to bills and claims, and invoice templates there. With this permission the app can only see files it created or that you opened with it. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
Google Sheets
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets		 The app keeps your invoice ledger in a spreadsheet in your own Drive (tabs for the ledger, clients, goals, bills, business profiles, settings, project costs, client expenses, and analysis). It reads and writes only that spreadsheet: the one it creates for you, or the one whose link or ID you give it in Connections. It does not open your other spreadsheets.
Google Calendar, read only
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly		 When you search your calendar from the app, it looks up events matching the word you type and uses the event's title, date, and attendee names and emails to start an invoice. It never creates, changes, or deletes events.

The app talks to Google's APIs directly from your browser. Your Google sign-in token is kept in your browser's local storage so you are not asked to sign in on every visit. It expires after about an hour, and it is only ever sent to Google.

Where your data is stored

  • In your browser (local storage on your device): invoices, a copy of your ledger, clients, bills, goals, settings, AI assistant chat history and any AI provider keys you add, and your Google sign-in token.
  • In your own Google account, if you connect it: the ledger spreadsheet, and the PDFs and receipts in the "mooInvoicer" Drive folder. Gmail drafts the app creates stay in your Gmail.
  • Not on our servers. We run no server that receives or holds your invoices, ledger, or Google data.

Optional AI assistant

The app has an optional AI assistant. It is off until you add your own API key for an AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, or OpenRouter). When you send the assistant a message, your browser sends it straight to the provider you chose, using your key. So that it can answer questions about your business, that request includes your message, any images you attach, the current invoice, and a summary of your ledger, clients, goals, bills, project costs, and client expenses. If you use Google sync, that summary includes data kept in your Google Sheet, such as ledger rows and bill payments you accepted from a Gmail scan. Nothing is sent to an AI provider unless you send a message. We never receive these messages. The provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to what you send it.

Other services the page loads

  • Google Fonts and Google Identity Services (the Google sign-in library).
  • cdnjs (run by Cloudflare), which serves the library that turns invoices into PDFs.
  • Contact form: if you send us a message with the Contact developer button, the name, reply address, and message you type are passed to us through the FormSubmit form service. Nothing else is sent.

Like any website, these services receive basic request information such as your IP address and browser type. The app itself does not set cookies.

How long data is kept

  • Data in your browser stays until you delete it (see below) or your browser clears it.
  • Data in your Google Drive, Sheets, and Gmail stays there until you delete it. It is yours.
  • The Google sign-in token expires after about an hour, and is removed when you sign out.
  • Contact messages you send us are kept only as long as we need them to reply.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. We do not show ads or use your data for advertising. We do not use your data to train AI models. The only places your data goes are your own Google account and, if you choose to use it, the AI provider you set up.

How to revoke access and delete your data

  • Revoke Google access: sign out of Google in the app, or remove mooInvoicer at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app then can no longer reach your Google data.
  • Delete data in your browser: clear this site's data in your browser settings (often under Privacy, then site data for invoicer.moo.llc). This deletes everything the app saved on that device.
  • Delete data in your Google account: delete the ledger spreadsheet and the "mooInvoicer" folder from your Google Drive, and any drafts you no longer want from Gmail.

Google API Services User Data Policy

mooInvoicer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, data the app gets from Google APIs:

  • is used only to provide the features described above, which you can see and use in the app;
  • is not transferred to anyone, except as needed to provide those features at your request (such as the AI assistant you set up), to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale of the app with your notice;
  • is not used or transferred for advertising, including personalized or retargeted ads;
  • is not sold, and is not used to work out credit-worthiness or for lending;
  • is not read by any person, unless you ask us to for support and agree to it, it is needed for security or to comply with the law, or it has been aggregated and anonymized;
  • is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. If the app ever needs a new Google permission, Google will ask for your consent before it is granted.
