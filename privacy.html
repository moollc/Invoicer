Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

moo Invoicer ("the app") is a client-side invoicing tool that runs entirely in your browser at https://invoicer.moo.llc. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

No servers, no data collection

The app has no backend server. It does not transmit any of your data to the developer or any third party. All invoice data, settings, and preferences are stored locally in your browser's localStorage and never leave your device, except where you explicitly connect your own Google account (described below).

Google account access

You may optionally sign in with Google to enable the following features. Each feature uses only the minimum permissions required:

  • Gmail (gmail.compose):used to create draft emails with your invoice attached. The app never reads, sends, or deletes your emails.
  • Google Drive (drive.file):used to save invoice PDFs to a folder named "mooInvoicer" in your own Drive. The app can only access files it creates; it cannot read other files in your Drive.
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets):used to maintain an invoice ledger in a spreadsheet in your own Drive. The app only reads and writes to the ledger sheet it creates.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly):used to search your calendar events by keyword so you can pre-fill invoice details from an upcoming event. The app only reads events; it never creates, modifies, or deletes calendar entries.

Your Google OAuth token is stored temporarily in your browser's localStorage to avoid repeated sign-in prompts. It is never sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

What is stored locally

The following data is stored only in your browser's localStorage and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Invoice content (client details, line items, amounts)
  • Invoice ledger history
  • Client address book
  • AI chat history
  • App preferences and settings
  • Your Google OAuth token (temporary, expires automatically)

Clearing your browser's site data will permanently delete all of the above.

AI features

The app includes an optional AI assistant. If you configure an AI provider (such as Google Gemini or another service), your chat messages are sent directly from your browser to that provider using your own API key. The developer does not receive or store these messages.

Cookies

The app does not use cookies.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will be updated. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
